About Pet Paradise

Story

Pet Paradise started through the love for happy animals. Growing up with multiple dogs and cats I grew a love to see happy emotions reverberate off my beloved pets. Ever since I've wanted to spread that feeling to all homes with pets.

Mission

The mission is to share my product to all pet owning households to bring that same emotion I encountered with my own pets to others. Spreading customizable and fun products in order to bring happiness and love to all pets and their owners.
